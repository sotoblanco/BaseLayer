# Sketching Word Vectors on the 2D Plane

Build geometric intuition for cosine similarity.

### Your Task
1. Use the chalk palette and the **Vector Arrow tool** (`Arrow`) to draw the x and y axes on the green chalkboard.
2. Sketch the positions of **King [0.9, 0.1]** and **Queen [0.8, 0.3]**.
3. Draw the angle **theta** between them.
4. Click **Show Solution** (or **Submit Drawing**) to inspect what was expected and self-evaluate your sketch!
